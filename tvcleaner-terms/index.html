What the app does

The app helps you remove clutter from Android TV and Google TV devices that you own or are authorized to manage. It connects to your TV over your local network using Android's wireless debugging (ADB), and can disable or re-enable pre-installed apps, install and configure an alternative home-screen launcher, and apply themes.
